# Ex.No.10-Content Creation (Reports, Articles, Case Studies, etc.) Using Prompt Patterns

# Date: 10-09-2026
# Reg. No.: 212225060282

# Aim:
To demonstrate how various prompting techniques such as query decomposition, decision-making, answer engineering, fact-checking, tail generation, menu actions, and semantic filtering can be employed to create content such as reports, articles, case studies, or creative works using ChatGPT or similar Large Language Models.

# AI Tools Required:
- ChatGPT
- Large Language Model (LLM)
- Web Browser
- Text Editor

# Explanation:

Prompt patterns are reusable structures used to guide Large Language Models to generate better and more controlled outputs.

In this experiment, two content-generation scenarios are selected and different prompting techniques are applied to create, refine, and evaluate the generated content.

The following prompt patterns are used:

1. Query Decomposition:
- Breaks a complex task into smaller and manageable sub-tasks.

2. Decision Making:
- Instructs the AI to evaluate multiple options and select an appropriate direction.

3. Answer Engineering:
- Specifies the required structure, format, depth, and organization of the response.

4. Fact Check List:
- Provides a checklist for verifying important factual claims before finalizing the content.

5. Tail Generation:
- Extends existing content logically while maintaining consistency and context.

6. Menu Actions:
- Provides multiple possible actions and allows the model to select or apply the appropriate action.

7. Semantic Filter:
- Controls the meaning, tone, style, relevance, and type of information included in the final output.

# Content Generation Scenarios Selected:

1. Educational Report on Renewable Energy
2. Artificial Intelligence Impact on Healthcare

# Scenario 1: Educational Report on Renewable Energy

## Basic Prompt:

"Write a report about renewable energy."

## First Draft Output:

"Renewable energy is energy obtained from naturally replenishing sources such as sunlight, wind, water, and biomass. It is an important alternative to fossil fuels because it can reduce dependence on non-renewable energy sources.

The major types of renewable energy include solar energy, wind energy, hydropower, biomass energy, and geothermal energy.

Renewable energy can help reduce environmental pollution and support sustainable development. However, challenges such as high initial costs, energy storage, and variable energy generation need to be addressed."

# Refined Prompt Using Query Decomposition:

"Create an educational report on renewable energy by following these steps:

1. Define renewable energy.
2. Explain the major types of renewable energy.
3. Describe their applications.
4. Explain their environmental and economic benefits.
5. Discuss major limitations and challenges.
6. Explain the role of energy storage and grid integration.
7. Provide a concise conclusion.

Organize the report using headings and subheadings. Use simple and educational language."

## Refined Output:

"RENEWABLE ENERGY

1. Introduction

Renewable energy refers to energy obtained from natural sources that are replenished continuously, including sunlight, wind, flowing water, biomass, and geothermal heat.

2. Types of Renewable Energy

2.1 Solar Energy
Solar energy uses sunlight to produce electricity through photovoltaic systems or heat through solar thermal systems.

2.2 Wind Energy
Wind turbines convert the kinetic energy of moving air into electrical energy.

2.3 Hydropower
Hydropower uses flowing or falling water to generate electricity.

2.4 Biomass Energy
Biomass uses organic materials such as agricultural residues and other biological materials to produce useful energy.

2.5 Geothermal Energy
Geothermal systems use heat from within the Earth for electricity generation or heating.

3. Benefits

- Reduces dependence on fossil fuels.
- Can reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
- Provides diversified energy sources.
- Supports long-term energy sustainability.

4. Challenges

- Some renewable sources are intermittent.
- Energy storage may be required.
- Initial infrastructure costs can be significant.
- Grid integration requires appropriate planning.

5. Conclusion

Renewable energy plays an important role in developing a more sustainable energy system. Continued improvements in technology, storage, infrastructure, and energy management can support its wider adoption."

# Decision-Making Prompt:

"Compare solar energy, wind energy, and hydropower for use in a general educational report. Consider availability, environmental impact, reliability, scalability, and common applications. Select the most suitable option to use as the main example in the report and explain the factors considered in the selection."

# Output:

"Solar energy is selected as the main example because it has broad applicability, can be deployed at different scales, and is easy to explain in an educational context.

Factors considered:
- Resource availability
- Scalability
- Environmental considerations
- Ease of deployment
- Common applications"

# Answer Engineering Prompt:

"Rewrite the renewable energy report using the following structure:

Title
1. Introduction
2. Types of Renewable Energy
3. Applications
4. Advantages
5. Challenges
6. Future Scope
7. Conclusion

Use formal educational language. Keep each section concise and logically connected. Use bullet points where appropriate."

# Fact Check List Prompt:

"Review the renewable energy report and create a fact-check list. Identify statements that require verification, distinguish general established information from specific claims, and flag any numerical or time-sensitive claims that should be supported by reliable sources."

# Semantic Filter Prompt:

"Refine the renewable energy report using the following semantic filters:

- Keep only educational and relevant information.
- Remove unnecessary repetition.
- Use a formal academic tone.
- Avoid unsupported numerical claims.
- Avoid exaggerated statements.
- Keep explanations understandable for engineering students."

# Final Version - Renewable Energy Report:

"RENEWABLE ENERGY

## Introduction

Renewable energy refers to energy obtained from natural sources that are continuously replenished. Major renewable sources include solar, wind, hydropower, biomass, and geothermal energy.

## Major Types

Solar energy converts sunlight into electricity or heat. Wind energy uses turbines to convert wind movement into electrical energy. Hydropower generates electricity using flowing or falling water. Biomass uses organic material as an energy source, while geothermal energy uses heat from the Earth.

## Applications

Renewable energy is used for electricity generation, heating, transportation-related applications, agricultural activities, and distributed power systems.

## Advantages

- Reduces dependence on finite fossil fuel resources.
- Supports diversification of energy sources.
- Can reduce emissions associated with conventional energy generation.
- Can support sustainable energy development.

## Challenges

- Solar and wind generation can vary with environmental conditions.
- Energy storage may be required for some applications.
- Infrastructure and grid integration can require significant planning.
- Renewable energy projects can have location-specific environmental and social considerations.

## Future Scope

Advances in energy storage, power electronics, smart grids, forecasting, and energy management can improve the integration of renewable energy into modern power systems.

## Conclusion

Renewable energy is an important component of sustainable energy development. Combining renewable generation with suitable storage, grid infrastructure, and efficient energy management can help address the challenges associated with its wider adoption."

# Scenario 2: Artificial Intelligence Impact on Healthcare

## Basic Prompt:

"Write an article about the impact of Artificial Intelligence on healthcare."

# First Draft Output:

"Artificial Intelligence is increasingly being used in healthcare. It can support medical image analysis, clinical decision-making, administrative tasks, drug discovery, and patient monitoring.

AI can help healthcare professionals process large amounts of information and identify patterns. However, challenges such as data privacy, bias, reliability, and ethical considerations must also be addressed."

# Refined Prompt Using Query Decomposition:

"Write an educational article about the impact of Artificial Intelligence on healthcare.

Break the task into the following sections:

1. Introduction to AI in healthcare.
2. Applications of AI in healthcare.
3. Benefits for healthcare professionals and patients.
4. Limitations and risks.
5. Ethical and privacy considerations.
6. Future possibilities.
7. Conclusion.

Use clear and formal language suitable for engineering students."

# Refined Output:

"ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN HEALTHCARE

## 1. Introduction

Artificial Intelligence refers to computational techniques that enable systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. Healthcare is one area where AI can support professionals in analyzing information and assisting with various processes.

## 2. Applications

AI can be used in areas such as:
- Medical image analysis
- Patient monitoring
- Clinical decision support
- Drug discovery research
- Healthcare administration
- Natural-language processing of medical information

## 3. Benefits

AI systems can help process large amounts of data and identify patterns. They can also automate repetitive administrative activities and support healthcare professionals in certain analytical tasks.

## 4. Limitations

AI systems can produce incorrect or misleading outputs. Their performance may depend on the quality and representativeness of the data used to develop them.

## 5. Ethical Considerations

Important considerations include patient privacy, data security, transparency, accountability, bias, and appropriate human oversight.

## 6. Future Scope

Future healthcare systems may increasingly combine AI with medical devices, electronic health records, medical imaging, and other digital technologies.

## 7. Conclusion

AI has significant potential to support healthcare, but its use requires appropriate validation, privacy protection, human oversight, and responsible implementation."

# Decision-Making Prompt:

"Consider the following healthcare applications of AI:

A. Medical image analysis
B. Administrative automation
C. Drug discovery
D. Patient monitoring

Select one application as the primary example for an educational article. Compare the options based on practical relevance, potential benefits, complexity, and ethical considerations. Explain the reasoning for the selection."

# Output:

"Medical image analysis is selected as the primary example because it provides a clear example of how AI can process complex visual information while also allowing discussion of accuracy, validation, human oversight, and ethical considerations."

# Answer Engineering Prompt:

"Rewrite the AI healthcare article using the following format:

Title
Introduction
Applications
Benefits
Risks and Limitations
Ethical Considerations
Future Scope
Conclusion

Use concise paragraphs and bullet points. Maintain a formal educational tone and avoid unsupported claims."

# Fact Check List Prompt:

"Create a fact-check checklist for the AI healthcare article.

Check whether:
1. Technical claims are reasonable.
2. Medical claims are not exaggerated.
3. Numerical claims have reliable sources.
4. Time-sensitive claims are identified.
5. Ethical and privacy statements are clearly presented.
6. The article distinguishes AI assistance from independent medical decision-making."

# Menu Actions Prompt:

"Review the generated healthcare article and choose the most appropriate improvement from the following menu:

A. Add technical details
B. Simplify the language
C. Add ethical considerations
D. Improve the conclusion

Select the action that would provide the greatest improvement for an educational audience and apply it."

# Output:

"Selected Action: C. Add ethical considerations.

Reason:
Ethical considerations are important when discussing AI in healthcare because the topic involves sensitive patient information, privacy, accountability, potential bias, and human oversight."

# Tail Generation Prompt:

"Continue the article by adding a section titled 'Responsible Use of AI in Healthcare'. Maintain the same formal educational tone and connect the new section logically with the previous discussion."

# Output:

"## Responsible Use of AI in Healthcare

Responsible implementation of AI requires appropriate validation, secure handling of patient information, transparency about system limitations, and human oversight. Healthcare professionals should remain involved in important decisions, particularly when AI-generated recommendations may affect patient care."

# Semantic Filter Prompt:

"Apply the following semantic filters to the article:

- Keep the content educational and objective.
- Remove exaggerated claims.
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.
- Do not provide medical advice.
- Maintain a formal academic tone.
- Keep the discussion focused on the impact of AI on healthcare.
- Clearly distinguish AI assistance from human medical decision-making."

# Final Version - Artificial Intelligence Impact on Healthcare:

"ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IMPACT ON HEALTHCARE

## Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is increasingly being explored for applications in healthcare. AI-based systems can process large amounts of information and assist with tasks such as medical image analysis, patient monitoring, administrative automation, and research.

## Applications

AI can support healthcare in several areas:

- Medical image analysis
- Patient monitoring
- Clinical decision support
- Drug discovery research
- Healthcare administration
- Natural-language processing

## Benefits

AI can assist healthcare professionals in processing large datasets, identifying patterns, automating repetitive tasks, and supporting certain analytical activities.

## Risks and Limitations

AI systems may produce inaccurate outputs and their performance can depend heavily on the quality and representativeness of the data used to develop them. Appropriate testing and validation are therefore important.

## Ethical Considerations

Important issues include:

- Patient privacy
- Data security
- Algorithmic bias
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Human oversight

## Responsible Use

AI should be implemented with appropriate validation and monitoring. Healthcare professionals should understand the limitations of AI systems and remain involved in important decisions.

## Future Scope

AI may continue to support healthcare research, medical imaging, monitoring systems, administrative processes, and other healthcare applications as the underlying technologies develop.

## Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence has the potential to support many areas of healthcare. However, effective implementation requires attention to accuracy, privacy, security, ethics, validation, and human oversight."
